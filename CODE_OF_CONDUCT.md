# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of Zuri, are committed to making participation in our project and community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We believe that collaboration, mutual respect, and open communication are essential to building a strong open-source community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward others.
- Welcoming different perspectives and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Showing empathy and respect toward other contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, threatening, or humiliating language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated hostile behavior.
- Any conduct that creates an intimidating or hostile environment.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, issues, and other interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Community communication channels.
- Other spaces where someone represents the Zuri project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

Reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly and confidentially.

Project maintainers will take appropriate and proportionate action in response to any behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers through the official reporting channel listed in the repository.

All reports will be handled with respect and confidentiality.
